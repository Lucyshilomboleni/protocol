Energy-Aware BLE Advertising for a Battery-Free Key Locator

Aim of the study
To design, implement, and evaluate through simulation an energy-aware BLE advertising protocol for a battery-free kinetic-energy-harvesting key locator, demonstrating its advantages over conventional static BLE configurations.

Research objectives

How the system works

StepWhat happens
1. MovementWalking, running or handling the keys produces energy in a small kinetic harvester.
2. StorageThe energy is stored in a 100 mF supercapacitor (1.8 V to 3.6 V).
3. DecisionThe energy-aware protocol chooses how often to send Bluetooth adverts.
4. AdvertisingThe tag sends adverts; a phone nearby receives them and finds the keys.
